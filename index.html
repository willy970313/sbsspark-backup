Privacy policy

Personal backup utility

A backup for my working files.

sbsspark-backup uses rclone to maintain a copy of the owner's Documents folder in the owner's personal Google Drive.

Purpose

Chi-Wei Wang operates this utility for personal backup and recovery. Scheduled jobs upload new and changed files and retain earlier versions according to the backup settings.

Google Drive access

The utility requests the drive.file permission to create and manage its backup files and folders. The owner grants access through Google's authorization screen.

Data handling

Backup data passes directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive. The utility stores its OAuth authorization information on the owner's computer for scheduled operation.

The privacy policy describes the data handled by the utility, its retention settings, and how the owner can revoke access.
