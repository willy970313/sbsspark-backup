Privacy policy

This policy describes how sbsspark-backup handles data for the account owner's personal backup workflow.

Operator and purpose

Chi-Wei Wang operates sbsspark-backup using rclone. The utility copies the owner's configured Documents folder to the owner's personal Google Drive and maintains those backups for recovery.

Information handled

The utility processes backup file contents and metadata, including file names, folder paths, sizes, modification times, and checksums used for synchronization and verification. It also uses OAuth authorization information to access Google Drive.

Google Drive permission

The utility requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It uses this permission to create, read, update, move, and delete its backup files and folders.

Use and disclosure

The utility uses data to create, maintain, verify, and restore the owner's backups. It transfers backup data to Google Drive under the owner's account. The utility has no advertising, analytics, or data-sale functions.

Storage and retention

Google Drive stores the current backup. When local files are removed or replaced, the utility moves their earlier backup copies into dated folders. These folders become eligible for removal after 90 days and are removed when a subsequent backup job runs successfully. Removed files then follow Google Drive's trash retention.

OAuth authorization information is stored on the owner's computer. Local backup logs are retained for 30 days. Access to stored files and credentials is governed by the owner's Google account settings and local filesystem permissions.

Owner control

The owner can revoke the utility's authorization in the Google account's third-party access settings. Existing backup files remain in Google Drive until removed under the retention settings or deleted by the owner.

This website

These pages contain static HTML and CSS, with no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website under GitHub's privacy statement.

Updates and contact

This policy will be updated if the utility's data handling changes. Questions can be sent to Chi-Wei Wang at willy970313@gmail.com.
